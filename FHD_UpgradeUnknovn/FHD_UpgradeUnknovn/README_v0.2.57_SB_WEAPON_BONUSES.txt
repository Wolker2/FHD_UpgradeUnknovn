FHD_UpgradeUnknovn v0.2.57 — SB weapon bonus replacement

Changes from v0.2.56:
- New SB generation no longer rolls Equipment Reliability.
- New SB generation no longer rolls Cargo Hook Maximum Speed or Minimum Speed.
- Added global random SB properties:
  * Weapon fire rate: SB III +3..5%, SB II +6..9%, SB I +10..15%.
  * Missile speed: SB III +50..100, SB II +110..180, SB I +190..300.
  * Missile weapon ammo capacity: SB III +1, SB II +2, SB I +3.
- All three can roll on any researched equipment/artifact and affect the corresponding equipped weapons globally.
- Bottom researched-item note changed to: "Возможность установить 2 ММ одновременно".
- Legacy definitions for removed properties remain only for save compatibility; they are not selected for new SB.
